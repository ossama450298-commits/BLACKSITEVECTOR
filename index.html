About

BLACKSITE: VECTOR is an original round-based tactical shooter in the spirit of turn-of-the-millennium PC shooters: low-poly models, baked lighting, hitscan weapons with real recoil, an economy and one life per round.

Two fictional private forces fight over a decommissioned freight compound. Raven carries the Vector Charge; Vanguard holds the line. Every model, texture, sound and line of music is generated by code at runtime.

Built with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and Three.js r128 (MIT). Version .
